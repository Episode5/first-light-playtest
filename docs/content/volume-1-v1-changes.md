# Volume 1 content proposal

All 66 recipes below are proposals for Ethan's review. All 131 existing recipes remain unchanged. The 100 concepts, their names and six starters are unchanged. Water + Human remains a deliberate no-reaction pair. Every new music recipe requires Sound, and every new rhythm recipe requires Music.

Prioritized reusable material and construction concepts (Ore, Metal, Wood, Tool, Tree, Forest, Wheel), then knowledge and communication paths, while repairing all 21 dead ends. No depth exceptions are needed. Only House moves earlier, from layer 7 to 6.
